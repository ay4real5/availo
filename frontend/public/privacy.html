Availo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Availo helps you find an earlier UK driving-test slot. It is a browser extension plus a companion web app and backend. This policy explains exactly what data we handle and where it goes. In plain terms: the sensitive details you'd use to book (your driving licence number and booking reference) stay on your own device and are never sent to our servers.

What we collect and why

  • Account details (email, password) — to create and secure your Availo account. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash on our backend; we never see your plain-text password.
  • Your alert preferences (test centre, current test date, search window, notification choices) — stored on our backend so we can alert you about matching slots.
  • Your driving licence number and booking reference — stored only in your own browser (local extension storage), used solely to fill the DVSA forms for you, like a password manager. These are never transmitted to Availo's servers.
  • Watch activity events — when the extension spots a slot, is blocked by DVSA, or is signed out, it sends a minimal event (test centre, time, timestamps) to our backend so we can send your alerts. It does not send the contents of the DVSA page.
  • Notification subscription — if you enable phone/desktop push, your browser's push subscription is stored on our backend so we can deliver alerts.

What we do NOT do

  • We never book, hold, or pay for a test on your behalf — you always do that yourself.
  • We never store your DVSA login credentials on our servers.
  • We never sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • The extension never tries to bypass DVSA's security checks; if it sees one, it stops.

Who we share data with (service providers)

  • Supabase — hosts our database (your account, preferences, events).
  • Render — hosts our backend service.
  • Resend — sends alert emails (receives your email address and the alert content only when an alert is sent).
  • Browser push services (e.g. Google/Mozilla/Apple) — deliver push notifications if you enable them.

Data retention & your control

You can clear your locally-stored licence/booking details any time from the extension's settings ("Clear saved details"). You can turn off notifications, stop watching, or ask us to delete your account and its data by contacting us.

Permissions the extension uses

It runs only on the DVSA "change your driving test" pages (*.gov.uk), uses local storage for your settings, and (optionally) notifications and background alarms so it can alert you and keep watching. It requests no access to your other browsing.

Contact

Questions or a deletion request: privacy@availo.app.

Availo is an independent tool and is not affiliated with DVSA or GOV.UK.
