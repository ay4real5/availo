Watching while you're away

While a tab is watching, Availo can gently refresh the DVSA page so new cancellations show up — so you can leave it running and get an alert on your phone.

⚠ Refreshing too fast can make DVSA flag your account and cancel your test. Keep this slow (90+ seconds). Availo stops immediately if DVSA ever shows a challenge, and never reserves a slot for you.

Best times to watch

Cancellations aren't random — they cluster. If you can't watch all day, these windows pay off most:

  • Right at 6am — DVSA reopens overnight and releases fresh slots. Availo pauses at night and auto-resumes then.
  • Weekday evenings — people rebooking free up their old slot.
  • About 3 working days before a date — DVSA's free-cancellation cutoff, when people drop tests they can't make.

Availo watches continuously while the tab's open — this is just where to concentrate if you're checking by hand.
